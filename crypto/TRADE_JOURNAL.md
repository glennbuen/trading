# Crypto Trade Journal — Wins, Losses, and What They Taught

A record of what actually happened after each trade action (entry,
stop-out, target hit, exit) — not just the plan, but the outcome and
the lesson. Companion to `WATCHLIST.md`. Ported structure from
`pse/TRADE_JOURNAL.md`/`us-stocks/TRADE_JOURNAL.md` — same discipline,
new market. Separate from TITA's own paper-trading ledger
(`paper_trading_state/*.json`) — this journal is for the real, $50,
discretionary OKX account only.

## How to log an entry

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — SYMBOL — ACTION (Entry / Stop-out / Target hit / Exit)
- **Price:** $X.XX
- **Setup at the time:** (tier, play type, what the read was)
- **What actually happened:** (thesis play out as expected? stopped on
  noise or a real break? ran past target?)
- **Lesson:** (repeat, adjust, or confirm/contradict the methodology)
```

Keep entries honest — a lesson from a loss is as valuable as one from
a win.
